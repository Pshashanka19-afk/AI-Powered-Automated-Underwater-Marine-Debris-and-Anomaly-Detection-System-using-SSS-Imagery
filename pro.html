About Qorvia

Qorvia is an advanced maritime AI system engineered for real-time detection, tracking, and risk assessment of synthetic ghost fishing nets and macro-debris across the world's oceans — for the next generation.

The platform integrates high-resolution satellite imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat), side-scan sonar, and autonomous maritime buoy telemetry to classify underwater hazards and alert cleanup vessels.

• Model: YOLOv11-OceanNet + Bathymetric Spectral Scanner
• Coverage: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian Ocean (Arabian Sea + Bay of Bengal), Arctic, Southern, Mediterranean
• Sensor Latency: < 2.4 seconds
• Status: Operational • Reserved @ 2026
